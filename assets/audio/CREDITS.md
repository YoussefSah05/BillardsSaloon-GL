# Bundled audio credits and licenses

Downloaded on 2026-10-09. These files are edited excerpts of downloaded audio,
not newly synthesised effects. `recipe.json` records original URLs, archive
members, source names, excerpt ranges, EQ and level adjustments. `bank.json`
records source and output SHA-256 hashes. Freesound clips use the public
high-quality MP3 previews; they are not the original lossless WAV files.

## Material foley and interface — CC0 1.0

- **Impact Sounds**, Kenney:
  https://kenney.nl/assets/impact-sounds
  `impactSoft_medium_000` through `_002`, `impactSoft_heavy_000` through
  `_002`, and `impactWood_light_000` through `_002`.
  Damped soft impacts substitute for cushion/rail contacts; light wood knocks
  substitute for pocket jaws. These are material foley, not dedicated pool
  cushion recordings. Cushion layers use different EQ and recorded takes.
- **Interface Sounds**, Kenney:
  https://kenney.nl/assets/interface-sounds
  `click_001`, `confirmation_001`, `error_001` for menu movement,
  confirmation and foul notifications. These are downloaded designed UI effects.
- **Fabric Rustling**, Iochi Glaucus:
  https://opengameart.org/content/fabric-rustling
  Three short fabric-friction excerpts for cue movement.

License: https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/

## General edits

Samples are converted to 48 kHz, mono, 16-bit PCM WAV. One-shots have their
leading silence removed and short boundary fades; peaks are reduced for mix
headroom. Loop boundaries are crossfaded. Source recordings retain their
original licenses; project source code licensing does not replace them.
